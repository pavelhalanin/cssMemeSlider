The meaning of the meme is a humorous photoshop in which, during the Presidential Medal of Freedom ceremony, Barack Obama appears as if he is awarding himself the title "Greatest President". The meme appeared after the U.S. president gave medals to several celebrities; later, a satirical online publication replaced the face of the honoree (Bruce Springsteen) with Obama's face and added a corresponding caption about self-promotion. Since then, the image has been used to ironically mock self-praise, giving oneself awards for minor achievements, or displaying narcissism.

The meme originates from a viral video filmed in 2008 at a school. The hero of the video - Nikita Litvinkov - wearing a blazer and a headband, completely seriously describes his lunch in the cafeteria. A few years later, the video became a source of quotes, and the final phrase about tea became especially popular. What he said: "There was a good borscht, with some cabbage, but not red. Just... Sausages. Well, there's also some strange salad with shredded carrots, cabbage, and apples with pineapples. Honestly, it pisses me off. And what else... Tasty tea, it quenches my thirst, and I feel like a human being!"

In 1998, the fourth episode of the second season of the series "Streets of Broken Lights" was released. One of the main characters, while on duty, came to a mental hospital, where he accidentally ran into an old acquaintance who had gotten a job as a janitor. What was said there:

- Hurry up, we're about to have lunch, cutlets

- With macaroni

- With mashed potatoes, with mashed potatoes

- We'll meet at lunch, well, take care

The meaning of the meme is to dramatically depict a state where things are not going according to plan, despair overwhelms you, or something so absurd happens that all you can do is clutch your head. This gesture perfectly conveys helplessness, horror, or a feeling of utter hopelessness, and most often the meme is used as a reaction to everyday, academic, or work-related situations.
